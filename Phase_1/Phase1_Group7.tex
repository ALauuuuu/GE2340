\section{Problem Definition \& Scope}
Currently, the campus dining resources are fragmented and unevenly digitized, with several canteens continuing to utilize physical menus and lacking a platform that allows for real-time access to campus dining menus. Moreover, the campus community encompasses diverse economic, cultural, and religious backgrounds. When choosing meals, students and staff weigh multiple factors based on their needs, such as budget, distance to the canteen, cuisine style, dietary preferences, religious restrictions, and food allergies, which poses a decision-making challenge. When facing these complex personal considerations, traditional manual searching is time-consuming and inefficient, often leading to unnecessary expenditure of effort. To address this problem, this project aims to introduce an artificial intelligence approach to build an efficient meal recommendation system.

The scope of this project includes a hybrid recommendation chatbot that uses an LLM to process natural language, extract the user intent, and perform data enrichment. It integrates this language processing with a rule-based scoring backend using structured menu databases to provide explainable recommendations.
The scope excludes restaurant foot traffic tracking or training a customized LLM from scratch.
